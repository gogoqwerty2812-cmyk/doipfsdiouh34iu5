Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data EgoLab collects when you use egolab.shop and place an order, why we collect it, who we share it with and what rights you have. We collect only what we need to deliver your order, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

EgoLab (“we”, “us”) runs the online store at egolab.shop and is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at egolab@mailfence.com or on Telegram @egolab_shop.

2. What data we collect

When you place an order

  • Delivery details: country, state (US only), city, postal code, street address and an optional delivery comment.
  • Contact details: the contacts you choose to give us — phone number, email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook or Signal. At least one is required so we can reach you about the order.
  • Order details: the products, quantities, prices, promo code, shipping cost, total and order number.
  • Payment details:
    • for crypto payments — the coin and network you chose, our receiving address and the transaction hash you enter (not needed for Monero);
    • for card payments — the payment status and amount from our payment partner. We never see or store your card number. If you gave us your email, it is passed to the payment partner so it can send you a receipt.
  • Your consent: the time you ticked the box agreeing to the processing of your data.

When you contact support

Your name, email, the subject and the text of your message.

When you browse the site

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers and do not set tracking cookies. Our hosting and network providers automatically process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job:

  • Delivery services — your delivery address, delivery comment and a contact such as your phone number (if given), so they can deliver the parcel.
  • Card payment partner (1pay.cx and the card processors it works with) — the order number and amount, and your email if you gave it. They process your card details under their own privacy policies.
  • Technical service providers that run the store for us: website hosting (GitHub Pages), network and security services and our payment server (Cloudflare), and the Telegram messenger, which we use as our internal inbox for new orders and support messages.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data and never pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

5. Data stored in your browser

To make the site work without an account, your browser keeps some data locally on your device: your cart, chosen promo code, language and your order history (so you can reopen your order page). This data stays on your device — you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address.

6. Crypto payments

Blockchain transactions are public and permanent by design: anyone can see a transaction between two addresses on most networks (Monero transactions are private). We use the transaction hash only to match your payment to your order.

7. How long we keep it

We keep order data for as long as needed to fulfil the order, handle questions, returns or warranty claims, and meet our legal and accounting obligations. After that we delete or anonymise it. Support messages are deleted once they are no longer needed. You can ask us to delete your data earlier — see section 9.

8. Security and transfers

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS, card payments are handled entirely by our payment partner, and access to order data is limited to the people who fulfil orders. Some of our service providers process data outside your country; where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer under applicable data protection law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK, Switzerland or some US states), you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already happened);
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email egolab@mailfence.com or write to us on Telegram, mentioning your order number if you have one. We reply within 30 days.

10. Children

Our store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of this page.
