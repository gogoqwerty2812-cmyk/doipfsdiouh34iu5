If something is wrong with your order, we will fix it. This page explains when you can return a product, what happens if an item arrives damaged or incorrect, and how refunds work for card and crypto payments.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

If a product arrives damaged, leaking, past its expiry date, or is not what you ordered, or something is missing from your parcel:

  1. Message us within 7 days of delivery at egolab@mailfence.com or on Telegram.
  2. Include your order number and photos of the item, the label and the packaging.
  3. We send a free replacement or give you a full refund for that item — your choice. You do not pay any shipping for this.

Changed your mind? 14-day returns

You can return products within 14 days of delivery for a refund, as long as they are:

  • unopened, with the original seal intact (supplements, powders, capsules and other consumables);
  • unused and in their original packaging (accessories such as belts, straps, shakers and bottles).

For health and hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns of supplements or other consumables once the seal has been opened, unless the product is faulty.

How to return

  1. Message us with your order number and the items you want to return. We reply with the return address.
  2. Send the items back within 14 days of telling us. Please use a tracked service — the return parcel is your responsibility until it reaches us.
  3. Return shipping is paid by you, unless the item was damaged, faulty or wrong.

Refunds

We refund within 14 days of receiving the returned items, or of approving your claim for a damaged or wrong item. If you return the whole order within 14 days, we also refund the original shipping cost.

  • Card payments — we contact you to arrange the refund; where the payment partner allows it, it goes back the same way you paid, otherwise we refund the same amount in USDT to a wallet you choose.
  • Crypto payments — refunded in the same coin and network, or in USDT, to an address you give us. The refund is the US dollar amount you paid, converted at the rate on the day of the refund.

Refunds are for the amount actually paid, after any promo discount.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any order that has not shipped yet — just message us with your order number and you get a full refund.

Your legal rights

This policy does not reduce any rights you have under the consumer law of the country you live in.
